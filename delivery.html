Delivery & Assembly

Our process from order confirmation to safe delivery.

Assembly and Delivery Stages

  1. Order Confirmation

    We confirm the selected configuration and delivery details with the customer.

  2. Component Preparation

    All selected components are checked and prepared for assembly.

  3. PC Assembly

    The components are carefully installed and connected inside the case.

  4. Testing and Packaging

    The computer is tested and securely packaged before shipment.

  5. Delivery

    The finished PC is delivered to the customer at the selected address.

Delivery Information

Before delivery, the system is checked to make sure the main components work correctly and the package is ready for transportation.
