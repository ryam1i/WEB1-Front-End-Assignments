About AsnkiPC

We build every PC to order: you choose the performance level, and we take care of the parts, the assembly and the testing.

Pick one of our three ready builds — Starter for 1080p esports, Pro for 1440p gaming and streaming, or Ultra for 4K and creative work — or describe your own on the Custom Order page.

Before dispatch, every system goes through 24 hours of CPU, GPU and RAM stress testing. All components are brand new and come with a warranty of up to 3 years, and your PC travels in form-fitted protective packaging with an insured courier.
